# Art the piece classifier was trained on

Chess Best Move recognizes a position with a small convolutional network trained on synthetic
screenshots. None of the art below is part of the app, is redistributed by it, or is drawn by it:
it was used locally to render training images, and only the trained weights ship. The sets whose
licenses ask for attribution are listed here with their authors and licenses.

Board images and piece sets from [lichess-org/lila](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila), taken at
commit `60acb9d51787f60fd4504b614d83114857010ed1`, with the authors and licenses as recorded in that repository's `COPYING.md`.
